Four studios, one quiet farm

Four private units that interlink in pairs, so families and friends can stay together.

Choose your studio

Every studio is private and self-catering, with its own patio opening onto the garden. Studios One and Two, and Three and Four, interlink, so you can book a pair as one family unit. The pool and braai area are shared.

  • Studio One: Queen bed, sleeps 2, from R1,400 per night. Opens into Studio Two to make a family unit for four.
  • Studio Two: Queen bed, sleeps 2, from R1,400 per night. Opens into Studio One to make a family unit for four.
  • Studio Three: Queen bed, sleeps 2, from R1,400 per night. Opens into Studio Four to make a family unit for four.
  • Studio Four: Queen bed, sleeps 2, from R1,400 per night. Opens into Studio Three to make a family unit for four.

In every studio:

  • Queen bed with quality linen and an electric blanket
  • Kitchenette with stove, microwave, fridge, toaster and air fryer
  • En-suite shower, towels and hairdryer
  • Air-conditioning, a safe and cleaning service
  • Free Wi-Fi, TV with Netflix and Prime Video
  • Pool, braai area and secure parking
