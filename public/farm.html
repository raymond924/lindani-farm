Life on the farm

A tree-lined road in, a mountain waiting at the end of it, and nothing to rush for.

A farm stay
made for slowing down

Lindani is a lifestyle farm in Klein-Drakenstein, on the edge of Paarl, with mountains on every side. Lemon trees, vineyards and olive trees spread across the land: usually the first thing guests notice when they arrive, and the last thing they smell on the way out.

Just down from the vineyards is the dam, and an unhurried pace that settles over the whole property. Keep an eye out for the farm's horses, geese and chickens.

“We've done a fair amount of travelling, and it's taught us how much a genuinely comfortable place to stay can make or break a trip. That's why we've tried to give Lindani the comfort of a good hotel, with the style and attention to detail that only comes from a personal touch.”

( John & Michelle, your hosts )
