How booking works

Every booking is made on NightsBridge, so the calendar is always up to date and there are never double bookings.

  1. ( 01 )

    Choose your dates

    Tap any "Book" button on this site to open NightsBridge and see which units are free.

  2. ( 02 )

    Book instantly

    Pick one unit, or two interlinking units for a family, and complete your booking on NightsBridge.

  3. ( 03 )

    Pay the deposit

    Pay 50% within 24 hours (or the full amount) using the details you receive. Stays less than 7 days away are paid in full.

  4. ( 04 )

    Pay the balance

    The balance is due 7 days before check-in.

  5. ( 05 )

    Arrive

    The day before, we WhatsApp your access code and directions. Check in from 14:00 to 17:00 (later by arrangement).

      Questions, answered

      How do I book?

      On NightsBridge, our booking calendar: tap any "Book" button on this site, choose your dates and units, and book instantly. Questions first? WhatsApp us on 063 678 2359.

      How do I pay?

      By EFT. Pay a 50% deposit within 24 hours of booking and the balance 7 days before check-in, or pay in full upfront. If your stay is less than 7 days away, full payment confirms it. Use your invoice number as the reference and send the proof of payment on WhatsApp or by email.

      What if I need to cancel?

      Cancellations must be in writing to info@lindanifarm.co.za. 30 days or more before arrival, nothing is forfeited; 21 days before, 25%; 14 days before, 50%; 7 days before, 100%. No-shows and early departures aren't refunded. Refunds are paid within 7–10 business days.

      Can I change my dates?

      Yes, up to 30 days before check-in, subject to availability. If the new dates have different rates, the difference is charged or refunded.

      What time is check-in and check-out?

      Check-in is from 14:00 to 17:00. Arriving later? Let us know in advance so we can arrange it (a fee may apply). Check-out is by 10:00. Early check-in and late check-out depend on bookings: ask us on the day.

      How does check-in work?

      It's self check-in. Once your stay is paid in full, we send your access code, parking details and directions on WhatsApp the day before you arrive. Park in the marked bays at the stone feature wall and walk down the stairs to the units.

      Is there a minimum stay?

      Two nights for stays that include a Friday or Saturday night. Shorter stays may be possible on other dates: just ask.

      Are meals included?

      Lindani is self-catering. Every unit has a kitchenette with a stove, microwave, fridge, toaster, air fryer, and tea and coffee, and there's a braai area with firewood provided.

      Can a family stay together?

      Yes. The units interlink in pairs (One with Two, Three with Four). Book both units of a pair and open the connecting door: a family of four gets two bedrooms, two bathrooms and two kitchenettes under one roof.

      Can we bring children?

      Yes. Each unit sleeps two (children pay the same rate as adults), and the units interlink in pairs to make a family unit for four. There's no lifeguard at the pool: children under 12 may not swim unaccompanied.

      Can we bring our dog?

      Sorry, pets aren't allowed. The farm has its own dogs, horses, geese and chickens.

      Can we swim in the dam?

      No, please don't swim in the dam, but you're welcome to walk down to the water. The pool is open from sunrise to sunset.

      Is there Wi-Fi and air-conditioning?

      Yes: free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning and a TV with Netflix and Prime Video in every unit, plus secure parking inside the farm gates.

      Can we book the whole farm?

      Yes. All four units together sleep eight, which suits families, friends and wedding guests. Parties and events in the units aren't allowed.
