How booking works

Every booking is made on NightsBridge, so the calendar is always up to date and there are never double bookings.

  1. ( 01 )

    Choose your dates

    Open NightsBridge from any "Book" button on this site and see which units are free.

  2. ( 02 )

    Book instantly

    Pick one unit, or two interlinking units for a family, and complete your booking on NightsBridge.

  3. ( 03 )

    Pay the deposit

    Pay 50% within 24 hours (or the full amount) using the details you receive. Stays less than 7 days away are paid in full.

  4. ( 04 )

    Pay the balance

    The balance is due 7 days before check-in.

  5. ( 05 )

    Arrive

    The day before, we WhatsApp your access code and directions. Check in from 14:00 to 17:00 (later by arrangement).
