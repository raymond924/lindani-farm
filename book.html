How booking works

No online payment needed: you pay by EFT once we've confirmed your dates.

  1. ( 01 )

    Send a request

    Choose your dates and send a request from this page, or WhatsApp us on 083 291 6790.

  2. ( 02 )

    We confirm

    We check availability and send your invoice with our banking details.

  3. ( 03 )

    Pay the deposit

    Pay 50% by EFT within 24 hours (or the full amount) and send us the proof of payment. Stays less than 7 days away are paid in full.

  4. ( 04 )

    Pay the balance

    The balance is due 7 days before check-in.

  5. ( 05 )

    Arrive

    The day before, we WhatsApp your access code and directions. Check in from 14:00 (latest 20:00).
